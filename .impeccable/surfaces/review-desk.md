# Review desk

Mode: Operate

## Direction contract

**Thesis.** Treat the intake queue as an accession register, with one clearly selected book and its metadata record alongside it.

**Own world.** Walnut and espresso surfaces, parchment text, and brass for selected records and approval. Serif titles and clothbound initials carry the library character. Thin rules align the queue and counts.

**Story.** The owner scans pending imports, chooses a book, compares metadata, verifies the destination, and approves.

**First viewport.** The sidebar anchors a compact status strip and search. The queue sits left of a wider record pane on desktop. On phones, the queue moves above the record. Selecting a book moves the brass spine marker and updates the record.

**Form.** Accession Ledger, direction 3, seed:7f5274ad67fc127a.

**Finish.** unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
